Observation vs. interpretation

What happened, and what you made of it — kept apart

Writing the two down separately makes it easier to come back later and ask: how sure am I about this, and how much does it matter? Confidence and significance are rated on their own scale, so a thought isn't automatically treated as fact.

How this fits with your care

Yours alone

Personal entries are scoped to your signed-in account. No one else can read them unless you choose to share your summary.

Not a diagnosis

Mind Timeline doesn't interpret your entries for you or generate clinical conclusions. It's a record, not a verdict.

Built alongside care

This works best as a companion to a psychiatrist, therapist, or care team — not as a replacement for treatment or emergency support.

Start with one entry.

You don't need a plan to begin — just something you noticed today. The timeline builds itself from there.

Open Mind Timeline

Mind Timeline is a personal tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you or someone you know is in crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line in your area.
